Mike finds shadcn/ui and HeroUI "fiddly and AI generated" and wanted a new default standard. Of four directions he chose A × B, "Instrument on paper" (2026-10-01): dense, annotated panels on warm paper, Manrope for everything including body copy, Modus violet in place of a cobalt accent, and a dark theme as well. He wants the primary action in bright Modus violet 500 in both themes, not the near-black violet 900 (DEC-014).
**Why it matters:** the direction is settled (DEC-002 to DEC-005, DEC-014). Proposing a stock kit or a new direction reopens a closed debate.
**What to do about it:** start from Modus Instrument and refine within it. Offer a new direction only when Mike asks for one.
