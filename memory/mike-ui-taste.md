Mike finds shadcn/ui and HeroUI "fiddly and AI generated" and wanted a new default standard. Of four directions he chose A × B, "Instrument on paper" (2026-10-01): dense, annotated panels on warm paper, one sans for everything including body copy (Manrope at first; since 2026-10-07 the brand fonts: Season Sans for everything, Season Serif only on headlines of 44px and up, because he finds the serif works only large, DEC-030), Modus violet in place of a cobalt accent, and a dark theme as well. He wants the primary action in bright Modus violet 500 in both themes, not the near-black violet 900 (DEC-014), and filled violet on every main action a reader can take (DEC-017).
On 2026-10-05, after scrubbing the first client hub for signs of an AI-made prototype, he took the system's own default devices out of everyday use: an eyebrow over every heading, two tones on every headline, FIG numbers nothing cites, dot grids everywhere, rows of stat tiles (DEC-015). He chose claims over labels, a drawing over a row of numbers, and the look kept as it renders (DEC-016, DEC-018).
**Why it matters:** the direction is settled (DEC-002 to DEC-005, DEC-014), and restraint is now part of it. Proposing a stock kit or a new direction reopens a closed debate; adding a device to every section brings back what he removed.
**What to do about it:** start from Modus Instrument and refine within it, following the book's Restraint section ([[restraint]]). Offer a new direction only when Mike asks for one.
