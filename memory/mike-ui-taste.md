Mike finds shadcn/ui and HeroUI "fiddly and AI generated" and wanted a new default standard. Of four directions he chose A × B, "Instrument on paper" (2026-10-01): dense, annotated panels on warm paper, Manrope for everything including body copy, and Modus violet in place of a cobalt accent. He asked for a dark theme as well.
On 2026-10-02 he asked to use more of the browser; a full-width version "lost some of its intentionality and flow". He chose a composed column that grows on big monitors instead (DEC-013). Composition and flow matter more to him than filling the screen. He wants the primary action in bright Modus violet (500) on Paper as well as Ink (DEC-014).
**Why it matters:** the direction is settled (DEC-002 to DEC-005, DEC-013). Proposing a stock kit or a new direction reopens a closed debate.
**What to do about it:** start from Modus Instrument and refine within it. Offer a new direction only when Mike asks for one.
