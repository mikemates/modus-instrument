On 2026-10-05 Mike checked Modus Instrument against its first client hub, which had just been scrubbed for signs of an AI-made prototype. Several signs were the system's own defaults, applied to every section whatever the content: an uppercase label over each heading, a grey second line on each headline, FIG numbers nothing cited, the dot grid behind every panel, rows of stat tiles, the same closing band on every page. He took the rules into the book (DEC-015) and chose how the demo and the starter show them (DEC-016, DEC-023).
**Why it matters:** a device that appears everywhere marks nothing, and readers take the page for a template rather than work made for them. That undercuts the analyst voice the system is for.
**What to do about it:** follow the book's Restraint section. Open a section on its claim, not a label. Keep two tones for the page's point of view. Number a figure only when the text cites it. Put the dot grid behind the opening panel only. Draw how numbers relate instead of tiling them. Close each chapter on its own question. Before adding a device, ask what it marks on this page.
